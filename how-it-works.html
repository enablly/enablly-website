Embedded first. Automated second.

Most automation fails because it is designed from outside the team that has to live with it. We do it the other way round: we embed with your team first — the forward-deployed way — and automate what we have actually seen.

01

1. Embed and observe

We join your team's daily rhythm for a short discovery: shadowing the work, reading the documents, mapping every hand-off. We are looking for the tasks that are repetitive, rule-based and frequent — the ones quietly eating your week.

02

2. Pick the worst bottleneck

You get a short list of candidate workflows with the friction in each made plain. Together we pick one. Small scope is a feature: it ships fast and proves itself in real use.

03

3. Build the micro-solution

We build an AI workflow for that one job: agents that gather, draft and check; connected to your tools through tool calling and MCP; grounded in your documents through retrieval (RAG); tested with evals before anyone relies on it.

04

4. Human checks, we refine

Your team reviews outputs in a simple checkpoint. Anything uncertain is flagged, not shipped. We refine until the team trusts it — then we pick the next bottleneck.

The result is not a platform to learn or a strategy to admire. It is hours handed back, every week, in the work your team already does.
