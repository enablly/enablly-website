AI Automation for Translation & Localisation

Expanding into new markets multiplies every piece of content by the number of languages. We automate translation and adaptation in parallel.

The situation

The website, listings and campaigns exist in one language. Each new market needs everything translated — and adapted, because literal translation reads wrong.

Where the time goes

Sending files to translators, waiting, checking terminology, and repeating for every update.

The micro-solution

Translation agents (Claude, Gemini, DeepL) render each text into all target languages in parallel, using your glossary so product terms stay consistent, and adapt tone and units for each market. A human reviewer checks the important pieces before they go live.

How it helps

Every market gets content at the same time, terminology stays consistent, and updates stop being a translation project.

What changes day to day

Publishing in five languages takes the same meeting as publishing in one.

Tools used

Claude Gemini DeepL

Quick questions

Is the quality publishable?

With your glossary and human review on key content, yes — and reviewers only check, never translate from scratch.

Which languages?

Any combination your markets need.
