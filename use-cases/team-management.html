AI Automation for Team Management

Managers lose hours to coordination overhead. We automate assignments, gentle chasing and status summaries across the team.

The situation

Work is agreed in meetings and chats, then scattered. The manager becomes the system: remembering who owes what and chasing updates one by one.

Where the time goes

Writing up tasks, assigning them, asking for updates, compiling status for the next meeting.

The micro-solution

An agent turns meeting outcomes and messages into assigned tasks in your tool, chases owners politely at the right intervals in parallel, and compiles a status summary before your review. Exceptions — anything overdue or blocked — are flagged to you, not hidden.

How it helps

Nothing quietly drops, and the manager's week stops being a chase list.

What changes day to day

You read one summary and handle the exceptions; the routine follows itself up.

Tools used

Notion Asana Microsoft Planner Slack alternatives

Quick questions

Will my team feel spammed?

Chasing follows intervals you set, in a tone you approve.

Which task tools?

The common ones — the workflow adapts to yours.
