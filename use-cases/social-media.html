AI Automation for Social Media

Social media is a treadmill of small tasks. We automate the drafting, designing and scheduling so a month of content is one review session.

The situation

A small team must post consistently on several platforms, but ideas, visuals, captions and scheduling all compete with their real jobs.

Where the time goes

Brainstorming posts, adapting one idea per platform, making visuals, writing captions and hashtags, and loading a scheduler.

The micro-solution

An agent plans a month of post ideas from your pillars, drafts captions per platform, generates matching visuals, and loads everything into the scheduler as drafts. A human approves the calendar in one pass.

How it helps

Consistency stops depending on someone's spare hour, and every post is reviewed before it can go live.

What changes day to day

One approval session a month replaces daily scrambling.

Tools used

Claude Midjourney Canva scheduling tools

Quick questions

Does anything post without approval?

Never — every post sits in drafts until a human approves it.

Which platforms?

Wherever you already post; the workflow adapts format per platform.
