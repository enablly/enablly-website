AI Automation for Finance Admin

Month-end reconciliation is matching hundreds of lines by eye. We automate the matching and leave humans the exceptions.

The situation

Bank lines, invoices and receipts live in three places. Reconciling them is slow, and a single mismatch can hide for an hour.

Where the time goes

Line-by-line matching, hunting missing receipts, and re-checking totals.

The micro-solution

An agent matches transactions to invoices and receipts by amount, date and reference; matched items are reconciled, near-matches are proposed, and true exceptions are listed with the evidence. Your finance person approves the proposals and works the exceptions.

How it helps

Most of the book matches itself, and human time goes only to the lines that genuinely don't fit.

What changes day to day

Month-end stops being a weekend.

Tools used

Claude your accounting software spreadsheets

Quick questions

Does it post to our accounts?

Entries are prepared and approved by your finance person before anything is final.

What about errors?

Every match shows its evidence, and uncertain pairs are never forced.
