AI Automation for Meetings

Meetings produce decisions that evaporate. We turn every recording into notes, actions and follow-ups without anyone typing them up.

The situation

Calls end, everyone remembers a different version, and writing up notes and actions is the job nobody does.

Where the time goes

Transcribing, summarising, extracting actions, and chasing the follow-ups afterwards.

The micro-solution

The recording is transcribed (Fireflies, Otter), an agent (Claude) summarises it into decisions and action items with owners, and the confirmed notes and tasks are sent to the team and your task tool.

How it helps

Every meeting leaves a written record and owned actions, and follow-up stops depending on memory.

What changes day to day

You confirm a summary in one minute instead of writing one in thirty.

Tools used

Fireflies Otter Claude your task tool

Quick questions

Which meeting tools?

Recordings from Zoom, Teams or Meet all work.

Is recording consent handled?

You record under your own policies; the workflow starts from recordings you lawfully have.
