AI Automation for Reporting & Dashboards

Reports are the same work every week: pull, paste, chart, comment. We automate the whole chain and deliver a report that explains itself.

The situation

Every Monday someone exports numbers from several platforms, pastes them into a deck or sheet, rebuilds charts and writes "what happened" from memory.

Where the time goes

Exporting and cleaning data, rebuilding the same charts, and writing commentary under time pressure.

The micro-solution

An agent pulls the numbers from your sources on schedule, builds the dashboard charts, compares against the baseline and drafts the commentary — what moved, by how much, and the likely why. A human checks before it circulates.

How it helps

The report arrives on time, numbers come from one consistent pull, and commentary is drafted while the data is fresh.

What changes day to day

Monday's report is a review task, not a construction project.

Tools used

Microsoft Copilot Gemini Claude Looker Studio

Quick questions

Where do the numbers come from?

Only your sources — ads platforms, CRM, sheets — pulled the same way every time.

Can it match our format?

Yes, your report layout is the template.
