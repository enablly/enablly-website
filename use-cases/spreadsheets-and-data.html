AI Automation for Spreadsheets & Data

Every business runs on spreadsheets, and every spreadsheet needs cleaning. We automate the cleaning, matching and merging.

The situation

Data arrives from forms, suppliers and systems in different formats. Someone spends hours standardising columns, removing duplicates and matching records by eye.

Where the time goes

Reformatting, de-duplicating, VLOOKUP-style matching, and fixing the same errors every week.

The micro-solution

An agent ingests each file, standardises formats, removes duplicates, matches records across sources and flags the rows it cannot resolve. You review only the exceptions.

How it helps

Clean data in minutes, and human attention spent only where judgement is actually needed.

What changes day to day

The weekly data chore becomes a short exception list.

Tools used

Claude Microsoft Copilot Google Sheets Excel

Quick questions

What if it matches wrongly?

Uncertain matches are always flagged for you, never guessed.

What formats?

Excel, CSV and Google Sheets in most setups.
