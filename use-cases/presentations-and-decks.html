AI Automation for Presentations & Decks

Decks eat senior hours. We automate the assembly — structure, layout, charts — so people start from a polished draft, not a blank slide.

The situation

Before every review or pitch, someone rebuilds a deck: pulling numbers, copying last month's slides, fixing layouts at midnight.

Where the time goes

Gathering data, structuring the story, building charts, and endless formatting fixes.

The micro-solution

An agent takes your bullets and data, structures the story, builds the deck in your template with charts drawn from the numbers, and writes speaker notes. You refine the message, not the margins.

How it helps

The first draft is 80% there, numbers match the source, and the template is never broken.

What changes day to day

Deck prep becomes an editing task measured in minutes.

Tools used

Microsoft Copilot Claude PowerPoint Google Slides

Quick questions

Does it use our template?

Yes — your deck template and styles are fixed inputs.

Are the numbers reliable?

Charts are drawn directly from the data you supply, and you review before presenting.
