AI Automation for Content Creation

Consistent content wins, but drafting from zero every day burns out the people who should be thinking. We build a workflow that turns one idea into a week of drafts in your voice.

The situation

A founder or marketer knows what they want to say, but the blog, LinkedIn, newsletter and social queue each need their own version, written from scratch.

Where the time goes

Re-explaining the topic for every channel, drafting, cutting down, expanding, and keeping the voice consistent across all of it.

The micro-solution

One source — a voice note, a call transcript, a rough draft — is transcribed and given to writing agents (Claude, OpenAI) briefed with your voice guide. They draft each channel's version; you edit and approve in one sitting.

How it helps

The blank page disappears, your voice stays consistent, and one idea does a week of work.

What changes day to day

Instead of writing five pieces, you review five drafts over coffee.

Tools used

Claude OpenAI Perplexity Notion

Quick questions

Will it sound like us?

The workflow is briefed with your voice guide and examples, and you approve everything before it ships.

Who owns the ideas?

You do — the system works from your input, not generic topics.
