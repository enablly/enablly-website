AI Automation for Proposals & Quotations

Proposals are high-value and slow. We automate the drafting so a tailored, client-ready proposal is ready for your review the same day.

The situation

Every enquiry triggers the same scramble: find the last similar proposal, update the details, rebuild the pricing table, chase internal approvals.

Where the time goes

Copy-paste from old documents, re-typing client details, recalculating prices, formatting.

The micro-solution

An agent takes the enquiry and your templates, pulls the right sections from past proposals, inserts the client's details, builds the pricing from your rate structure, and produces a polished draft. You review, adjust and send.

How it helps

Proposals go out while the conversation is warm, priced consistently and formatted properly every time.

What changes day to day

Drafting a proposal becomes reviewing one.

Tools used

Claude Microsoft Copilot your document templates

Quick questions

Does it invent prices?

No — pricing comes only from your rate structure and rules.

Can it match our format?

Yes, your templates define the output.
