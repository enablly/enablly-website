AI Automation for Paid Ads

Ad accounts generate endless small chores. We automate variants, checks and reporting so the media buyer spends time on strategy, not screenshots.

The situation

Campaigns run on Meta and LinkedIn, but making creative variants, checking budgets and building weekly reports eats the week.

Where the time goes

Writing ten headline variants, screenshotting results, pulling numbers into a report, watching budgets by hand.

The micro-solution

Agents generate ad copy and creative variants from your best performers, watch spend against your rules and flag anomalies, and assemble the weekly performance report automatically. Budget changes and launches always need your approval.

How it helps

More variants tested, problems flagged early, and reports that write themselves.

What changes day to day

The media buyer opens a summary and a variant queue instead of twelve dashboards.

Tools used

Claude OpenAI Meta Ads LinkedIn Ads

Quick questions

Will it change budgets on its own?

No — it flags and recommends; a human approves every spend decision.

Which platforms?

Meta and LinkedIn in most setups; the pattern works wherever reporting exists.
