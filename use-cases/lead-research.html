AI Automation for Lead Research

Good outreach starts with a good list, and building one by hand is slow. We automate the research and deliver clean, checked lists.

The situation

A sales team needs accounts that match its ideal customer profile, with the right people and contact details — and a rep can burn a day building fifty rows.

Where the time goes

Searching directories and LinkedIn, checking company fit, finding decision-makers, verifying details, de-duplicating.

The micro-solution

Research agents sweep the web and your data sources for companies matching your criteria, identify the relevant people, verify and de-duplicate the records, and deliver a clean list in your format.

How it helps

Reps sell instead of searching, and the list arrives checked rather than hopeful.

What changes day to day

Every morning starts with a fresh, ready list.

Tools used

Perplexity Claude LinkedIn data providers

Quick questions

How do you define a good lead?

Your criteria lead — industry, size, region, role — and every record is checked against them.

What format do we get?

Your format: spreadsheet or straight into your CRM.
