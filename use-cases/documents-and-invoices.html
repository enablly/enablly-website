AI Automation for Documents & Invoices

Documents arrive as PDFs, scans and photos, and someone re-types them into systems. We automate the reading, checking and filing.

The situation

Invoices, receipts, contracts and forms land in an inbox. A person opens each one, types the details into accounting or a spreadsheet, checks totals and files it.

Where the time goes

Data entry, total-checking by calculator, chasing missing fields, and filing into the right folders.

The micro-solution

Computer vision reads each document and extracts the fields. An agent sorts by type — invoices to accounting with totals verified, receipts to expenses, contracts to their folder with key dates noted. Anything unreadable or mismatched is flagged for a human.

How it helps

No more re-typing, totals are checked every time, and documents file themselves.

What changes day to day

Your admin reviews a short exception list instead of a tall inbox.

Tools used

Claude OpenAI your accounting software

Quick questions

What about messy scans?

The reader handles most scans and photos; unreadable items are flagged, never guessed.

Which systems?

Entries go to your accounting or spreadsheet setup.
