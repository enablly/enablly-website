AI Automation for Customer Support

Support queues are mostly the same questions wearing different words. We automate the common answers and route the rest to humans faster.

The situation

Tickets pile up across chat, email and social. Most ask the same twenty questions, but billing and urgent cases wait in the same queue.

Where the time goes

Reading and sorting every message by hand, copying known answers, and hunting for the urgent ones.

The micro-solution

A triage agent classifies every message by intent. Common questions are answered automatically from your help content (RAG over your docs, OpenAI/Claude). Billing questions are prepared with the customer's HubSpot/Zendesk record attached for a human to finish. Urgent cases jump the queue to a person immediately.

How it helps

Customers with simple questions get instant answers, and agents spend their time on the cases that need a human.

What changes day to day

The queue your team opens is shorter, sorted, and half-answered.

Tools used

OpenAI Claude HubSpot Zendesk

Quick questions

Will customers know it's AI?

That's your choice — the setup follows your disclosure policy.

What about angry customers?

Sentiment and urgency route them to humans first, not last.
