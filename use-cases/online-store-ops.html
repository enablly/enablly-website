AI Automation for Online Store Ops

Running a store is a hundred small updates a day. We automate listings, stock sync and order watching so the store runs itself between your checks.

The situation

Products change, stock moves and orders flow across a store and marketplaces. Keeping every listing accurate by hand means some are always wrong.

Where the time goes

Writing and updating listings, copying stock numbers between systems, and spotting problem orders late.

The micro-solution

An agent drafts and updates listings from your product data (titles, descriptions, images from the product-photo workflow), syncs stock across channels, and watches orders for issues worth flagging. Changes are prepared for your approval where they matter.

How it helps

Listings stay accurate everywhere, stockouts and oversells surface early, and store admin shrinks to a review queue.

What changes day to day

You run the store from an exceptions list instead of a spreadsheet marathon.

Tools used

Shopify marketplaces Claude

Quick questions

Which platforms?

Shopify and major marketplaces in most setups.

Does it change prices?

Only under rules you set, and significant changes are flagged for approval first.
