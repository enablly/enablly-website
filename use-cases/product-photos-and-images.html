AI Automation for Product Photos & Images

Good product images sell, and photoshoots are slow and expensive. We turn phone photos into clean, consistent, listing-ready images.

The situation

A seller photographs products on a phone, then spends hours removing backgrounds, fixing light and making marketplace-specific versions.

Where the time goes

Manual background removal, inconsistent backdrops, resizing and re-exporting for every channel.

The micro-solution

Computer vision cuts the product out; generative AI (Adobe Firefly, Midjourney) creates clean or lifestyle backdrops that fit the brand; every channel's size and format is exported automatically. A human picks the finals.

How it helps

Studio-look images without a studio, and a consistent catalogue across every channel.

What changes day to day

New products are listed the same day they are photographed.

Tools used

Adobe Firefly Midjourney Canva

Quick questions

Do the products stay accurate?

The product itself is never regenerated — only the backdrop — and you approve every image.

What photos do we need?

Ordinary phone photos with decent light.
