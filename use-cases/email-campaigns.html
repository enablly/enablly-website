AI Automation for Email Campaigns

Campaigns stall at the same three chores: segmenting the list, writing variants, and building the send. We automate all three.

The situation

A list sits in one big bucket, so every email is written for everyone — and still takes a day to segment, draft and set up.

Where the time goes

Cleaning and slicing the list, rewriting the same email for different audiences, and fiddly campaign setup.

The micro-solution

An agent segments the audience by behaviour and profile, drafts a version per segment in your voice, and builds the campaign ready to send. A human approves the copy and presses send.

How it helps

Each audience gets a relevant version, and a campaign is an afternoon's review instead of a week's project.

What changes day to day

You approve a prepared campaign instead of assembling one.

Tools used

Claude HubSpot Mailchimp Brevo

Quick questions

Does it send by itself?

No — sends are prepared, never fired, without your approval.

Can it use our platform?

It works with the major email platforms and CRMs.
