AI Automation for CRM Cleanup & Updates

Every CRM quietly rots: duplicates, gaps, stale records. We build a checker that keeps it clean continuously instead of once a year.

The situation

Reps stop trusting the CRM because the same company appears three times, key fields are empty, and nobody knows which record is real.

Where the time goes

Manual de-duplication, chasing missing fields, and fixing data one record at a time.

The micro-solution

A checker agent sorts every record into three paths: duplicates are prepared for merging, missing fields are filled from the web and your documents, and clean records pass through. Merges and overwrites are human-approved.

How it helps

One true record per account, fields filled without data-entry hours, and a CRM the team trusts again.

What changes day to day

Cleanup becomes a small daily review queue instead of an annual disaster project.

Tools used

HubSpot Salesforce Clay

Quick questions

Could it merge the wrong records?

Merges are drafted, not executed — a human approves each batch.

Which CRMs?

HubSpot and Salesforce in most setups.
