AI Automation for Video Creation

Every platform wants video, and every video wants editing. We automate the repetitive middle — cutting, captioning, resizing — so a raw clip becomes a week of posts.

The situation

A team records good raw footage — product demos, founder clips, event video — but publishing stalls because editing each clip for each platform takes hours.

Where the time goes

Transcribing, cutting dead air, adding captions, resizing for each platform, writing titles and descriptions.

The micro-solution

Speech-to-text transcribes the footage; an agent finds the strongest moments and assembles cuts with Runway and HeyGen where a presenter is needed; captions, sizes and titles are generated per platform; a human reviews before anything posts.

How it helps

One recording becomes many finished videos, captions are never skipped, and every platform gets its correct format.

What changes day to day

The team records once and reviews a queue of finished drafts instead of living inside an editor.

Tools used

Runway HeyGen ElevenLabs CapCut

Quick questions

Do we need a studio?

No — phone footage is fine; the workflow cleans and formats it.

Can it make presenter videos?

Yes, with HeyGen avatars where that fits, always human-approved.
