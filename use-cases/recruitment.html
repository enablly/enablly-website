AI Automation for Recruitment

Hiring managers drown in CVs. We automate the screening and admin so your time goes to interviews, not sifting.

The situation

A role opens, a hundred CVs arrive, and someone reads all of them against the same criteria — slowly, and inconsistently by Friday.

Where the time goes

Reading CVs, checking must-have criteria, building a shortlist, and scheduling admin.

The micro-solution

An agent reads every CV against your criteria, sorts applicants into shortlist, maybe and no piles with reasons shown, and prepares interview scheduling. Every decision is reviewable — a human makes the final call on who advances.

How it helps

Every applicant is screened against the same standard, fast, and the shortlist arrives with its reasoning visible.

What changes day to day

You interview a prepared shortlist instead of reading a pile.

Tools used

Claude OpenAI your ATS or inbox

Quick questions

Does the AI reject people on its own?

No — it sorts and explains; humans decide who advances.

Is it fair?

Every CV is measured against the same written criteria, and decisions are auditable.
