AI Automation for Onboarding & Training

Onboarding is twelve small tasks in four systems, repeated for every hire. We automate the checklist so nobody's first week depends on memory.

The situation

A new hire is confirmed, and someone must create accounts, send documents, assign training and chase completions — while doing their actual job.

Where the time goes

Repetitive setup across tools, sending the same documents, tracking who finished what.

The micro-solution

A workflow runs the onboarding checklist automatically: accounts and documents prepared, training modules assigned (Notion), progress tracked, and the manager gets a readiness summary. The manager checks; the new hire arrives to everything ready.

How it helps

No step is forgotten, every hire gets the same quality of start, and progress is visible without chasing.

What changes day to day

Onboarding becomes a checklist that runs itself, with a human watching the dashboard.

Tools used

Notion your HR and IT tools

Quick questions

Which tools does it connect?

Your HR, document and training tools — the checklist adapts.

What does the manager do?

Reviews one readiness summary instead of chasing twelve tasks.
