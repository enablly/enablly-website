AI Automation for Research & Market Intel

Nobody has time to watch the market every day, so nobody does. We build a watcher that reads it for you and briefs you weekly.

The situation

Competitors change prices, launch products and publish content, and the team finds out late — from a customer.

Where the time goes

Manually checking competitor sites, scanning news, and assembling findings into something readable.

The micro-solution

Research agents (Perplexity, Gemini) monitor your competitors and market sources on schedule, note what changed, and compile a short weekly brief with sources linked. You read five minutes of signal instead of hours of noise.

How it helps

Changes surface within days, with the evidence attached, and the brief is written for decisions, not for archives.

What changes day to day

Market awareness becomes a weekly five-minute read.

Tools used

Perplexity Gemini Claude

Quick questions

What sources can it watch?

Public websites, news and the sources you nominate.

How often?

Weekly briefs are typical; urgent topics can be daily.
