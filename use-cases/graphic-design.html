AI Automation for Graphic Design

Designers lose their best hours to resizing, re-laying-out and starting from blank pages. We build a small AI workflow that turns a brief into on-brand first drafts and every size variation, so designers spend their time designing.

The situation

A marketing team needs a steady flow of social posts, banners and campaign visuals. Briefs arrive as messages and documents; the designer starts each one from scratch and then remakes it in five sizes.

Where the time goes

Hunting for brand assets, generating a first concept, adapting one design to every format, and rounds of small text and logo fixes.

The micro-solution

An agent reads the brief, generates concept visuals with Adobe Firefly and Midjourney inside the brand palette, builds the layout in Canva/Figma, then produces every size variant automatically. The designer picks and polishes.

How it helps

First drafts appear in minutes, variants make themselves, and the brand look stays consistent because the palette and templates are baked in.

What changes day to day

The designer opens a folder of ready drafts instead of a blank canvas, and spends the day on the ideas that need taste.

Tools used

Adobe Firefly Midjourney Canva Figma

Quick questions

Will it match our brand?

Yes — your palette, fonts and templates are fixed rules in the workflow, and a designer approves every final piece.

What do we need to start?

Your brand assets and a few recent briefs.
