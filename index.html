How the technology works, in plain words.

Agentic AI — Instead of one question and one answer, AI agents carry a small job from start to finish: gather, draft, check, hand over.
Multi-agent orchestration — Several agents split a bigger workflow between them, each doing the stage it is best at, like a small team.
Large language models (LLMs) — The technology behind tools like ChatGPT and Claude. It reads, writes and reasons over text — the raw material of most office work.
Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) — The AI answers from your documents and data, not from guesswork, so outputs match your facts and your wording.
Tool calling and MCP — The AI can use your actual software — CRM, inbox, spreadsheets, store — instead of just talking about it.
Copilots — AI assistance inside the tools your team already opens every day, helping while they work.
Computer vision — AI that reads images and documents: product photos, invoices, receipts, screenshots.
Speech-to-text and voice — Meetings and voice notes become text that can be summarised, searched and acted on.
Text-to-image and video — New visuals and clips generated and adapted for designs, posts and listings.
Evals and guardrails — Every build is tested against real examples and fenced with rules, so it stays accurate and on-brand.
Human-in-the-loop — A person checks the important outputs before anything reaches a customer. Always.

FAQ

What does an AI automation specialist actually do?

We find the repetitive, manual work inside a team — copying data, drafting the same documents, chasing updates, cleaning lists — and build small AI tools that do that work, with a human checking the results. You keep the judgement calls; the machines take the repetition.

What does "forward deployed" mean?

It means we work inside your team while we build, not from outside. We learn the real workflow first, then build for it. It is how the solution ends up fitting your day instead of forcing your day to fit the solution.

Do you replace our staff or our existing tools?

Neither. The micro-solutions plug into the tools you already use and take the repetitive parts off your people's plates. Your team keeps the decisions, the relationships and the final say.

How big is a typical project?

Deliberately small. One workflow at a time — a micro-solution. It proves itself in daily use, then we pick the next bottleneck together.

Is our company data safe?

Builds run inside your accounts and permissions wherever possible, outputs are human-checked, and guardrails limit what the AI can touch. We scope data access with you before anything is built.

Which tools and AI models do you work with?

Most major ones — see the list above. We are independent and tool-agnostic, so the choice follows your stack and the job, not our partnerships.
